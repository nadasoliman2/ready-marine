Returns and Refunds Policy
Last updated: July 28, 2026
At Ready Car, we aim to provide high-quality products and reliable service. Should you need to exchange or return a product, please review the following policy, which is an integral part of the site's Terms and Conditions.
1. Scope of Application
This policy applies to all orders purchased through the readycar.store website.
2. Return and Exchange Conditions
A return or exchange request can be accepted if all the following conditions are met:
• The request is submitted within 14 days of the delivery date, or within the period specified by applicable law if longer.
• The product is in its original condition as received.
• The product has not been installed, used, modified, or repaired.
• The product is free from any damage, scratches, breaks, or signs of use.
• All original accessories, packaging, labels, and the invoice or proof of purchase are included.
Ready Car reserves the right to inspect the product before finalizing the return or exchange approval.
3. Non-Returnable and Non-Exchangeable Products
Products cannot be returned or exchanged in the following cases, unless there is a manufacturing defect or an error on Ready Car's part:
• Products that have been installed or used.
• Products damaged due to misuse or incorrect installation.
• Special orders or items custom-sourced at the customer's request.
• Electrical or electronic products after the packaging is opened or installed, unless a manufacturing defect is proven.
• Products that are broken or damaged after delivery.
• Products with removed labels or original packaging.
• Any product that does not meet the return and exchange conditions outlined in this policy.
4. Damaged or Incorrectly Sent Products
• If the customer receives a different product than ordered, or if the product arrives damaged due to a packing or shipping error, they must contact Ready Car within 48 hours of receipt, providing clear photos of the product and packaging.
• Upon verification of the error, Ready Car will cover the return or exchange costs, including related shipping fees.
5. Incompatible Vehicle Parts
• The customer is responsible for ensuring part compatibility with their vehicle before completing the purchase, including checking the VIN (Vehicle Identification Number) or Part Number, or any other technical data as needed.
• Incompatibility with the vehicle is not considered grounds for a return or exchange if the product matches the specifications ordered by the customer, unless the incompatibility is due to an error by Ready Car.
6. Return or Exchange Request Procedures
To request a return or exchange, please contact customer service and provide:
• Order number.
• Customer name.
• Reason for the return or exchange.
• Clear photos of the product when necessary.
The request will be reviewed, and the customer will be notified of the decision within a reasonable time.
7. Shipping Fees
• If the return or exchange is due to a Ready Car error or a proven manufacturing defect, Ready Car will bear the related shipping fees.
• If the request is based on the customer's preference or for a reason not attributable to Ready Car, the customer will bear all two-way shipping fees and any additional resulting charges, to the extent permitted by law.
8. Refunds
• Upon receipt, inspection, and approval of the returned product, the eligible amount will be refunded using the original payment method whenever possible, or through any other agreed-upon method.
• Refunds are processed within a reasonable time, though the timeframe for the funds to appear in the customer's account may vary depending on the payment provider or bank's procedures.
9. Rejection of Return or Exchange Requests
Ready Car reserves the right to reject any request if:
• The conditions stated in this policy are not met.
• There is evidence of use, installation, or damage to the product.
• Incorrect or misleading information was provided.
• It is proven that the reason for the request is not due to a Ready Car error or a product defect.
10. Warranty
• If the product is covered by a manufacturer's or supplier's warranty, claims will be processed according to those warranty terms. This policy does not affect any rights granted by the warranty or guaranteed by consumer protection laws.

1. نطاق التطبيق
تسري هذه السياسة على جميع الطلبات التي يتم شراؤها من خلال موقع readycar.store.
2. شروط الاستبدال والاسترجاع
يمكن قبول طلب الاستبدال أو الاسترجاع إذا توفرت جميع الشروط التالية:
• تقديم طلب الاستبدال أو الاسترجاع خلال 14 يومًا من تاريخ استلام المنتج، أو خلال المدة التي يحددها القانون الواجب التطبيق إذا كانت أطول.
• أن يكون المنتج في حالته الأصلية كما تم استلامه.
• ألا يكون قد تم تركيبه أو استخدامه أو تعديله أو إصلاحه.
• أن يكون المنتج خاليًا من أي تلف أو خدوش أو كسور أو آثار استخدام.
• أن تكون جميع الملحقات والتغليف الأصلي والملصقات والفواتير أو إثبات الشراء مرفقة مع المنتج.
ويحتفظ Ready Car بحق فحص المنتج قبل الموافقة النهائية على طلب الاستبدال أو الاسترجاع.

• المنتجات التي تعرضت للتلف بسبب سوء الاستخدام أو التركيب غير الصحيح.
• الطلبات الخاصة أو المنتجات التي تم توفيرها خصيصًا بناءً على طلب العميل.
• المنتجات الكهربائية أو الإلكترونية بعد فتح العبوة أو تركيبها، ما لم يثبت وجود عيب مصنعي.
• المنتجات التي تعرضت للكسر أو التلف بعد الاستلام.
• المنتجات التي أزيلت عنها الملصقات أو العبوات الأصلية.
• أي منتج لا يطابق شروط الاستبدال والاسترجاع المذكورة في هذه السياسة.
